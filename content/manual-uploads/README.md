# Manual featured photos

Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP here using GitHub's Upload files screen. Use a simple
filename such as `gola-bazar.jpg`. The upload must be on `main` before running
GlobeWire Manual Post. Enter the filename in `image_upload`, and fill in the
photo credit and permission or license. Leave `image_url` empty for an upload.

Use photos you are authorized to publish. Files committed here are public.
The publisher creates a separate 1280×720 JPEG for the finished article.
